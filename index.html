Planned and led the integration of five international acquisitions, adding $60M in revenue and building a 14-office global footprint.

Pulled 500+ employees across France, Norway, the Netherlands, Germany, Ukraine, and Russia into one company with a shared culture, single HRIS, an intranet, a shared knowledge system, exchange programs, and unified titles and salary bands. Voluntary turnover stayed under 10%, and we earned recognition from Inc. Magazine, Red Herring, and PACT.

When the war in Ukraine put our teams in Ukraine and Russia at risk, I led the relocation of 40 staff to safety and kept operations running through the disruption.

As COO, I ran the global senior leadership team, oversaw the build-out of our headquarters and main European offices, and owned executive goal-setting.

As CPO, I hired C-level executives, staffed large sales teams, and led reductions in force when the market required them.

Managed the business to a 10% EBITDA margin.
